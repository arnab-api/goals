Proposed caption. A sub-goal can remain represented while the neural states carrying it change. (a) A functional description connects a selected goal with its implementation and continued pursuit. (b) At three illustrative positions, the LM processes the available prefix, contributes selected head outputs to the computation, and predicts a next token. We capture those outputs separately at each position. The pre-implementation encoding is cached for injection into separate runs that describe the operation or implement it in another programming language. Comparing representations over positions investigates temporal persistence of goal-related information.

The snippets and readout are illustrative, not saved experimental outputs. Positions are selected snapshots, not a tokenizer-accurate trace. Shared color does not establish transmission between encodings or an unchanged stored goal. The figure leaves open which earlier latent states, instructions, and generated tokens contribute to later goal representations.
